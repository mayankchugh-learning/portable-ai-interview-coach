# Answer assessment — fresh 25-point rubric

This is a **new consistent rubric** for sessions after this pack is created. Historical best-attempt scores from prior coaching are included only as a qualitative baseline; do not compare point-for-point without knowing the original rubric.

| Category | 0–5 | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Directness / relevance | 5 | First sentence answers the exact question; no tangents |
| Technical/subject correctness | 5 | Correct layers, constraints, and factual limits |
| Ownership and trade-offs | 5 | Practical actions, accountable owners, security/governance boundaries |
| Evidence and decision | 5 | Observable validation and a definite next decision or go/no-go |
| Spoken clarity / concision | 5 | Natural short sentences, suitable length, minimal repetition |

Scoring guide: 0 absent; 1 significantly deficient; 2 partial; 3 satisfactory with omissions; 4 strong; 5 precise and complete for the question. Always give the score breakdown and one highest-leverage improvement, never a made-up official panel score.

Feedback template:
- Direct answer quality: ...
- Risk/owner/validation missing: ...
- Accuracy correction: ...
- New score: **x/25** [D / T / O / E / C].
- One change for the next attempt: ...
- Optional natural 45-second rewrite: ...
- Next: ask user whether to retry, move on or end.
