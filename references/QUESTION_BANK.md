# Rotating interview question bank — no automatic quiz

Pick one question per 15-minute session. The candidate must answer aloud in their own words. Questions are **practice prompts**, not evidence of previously asked or scored interview questions.

## Architecture, integration, cloud
1. How do you decide whether to introduce an API gateway rather than direct service calls?
2. Explain event-driven architecture and when an asynchronous queue is the wrong choice.
3. A retail store emits duplicate sales events. How would you prevent double processing?
4. How would you integrate an older on-premises system with a cloud SaaS platform?
5. What would you standardise across Azure and AWS without making the design unnecessarily complex?
6. A service's P95 latency doubles after a release. What do you decide first?
7. When would you choose eventual consistency over a distributed transaction?
8. Describe a rollback strategy for a high-risk production integration.

## GenAI, RAG, models and evaluation
9. RAG retrieved the right passage but the answer is wrong. Which layer failed?
10. How would you enforce document-level or tenant-level retrieval permissions?
11. When should a RAG system abstain instead of producing an answer?
12. How would you compare BM25, semantic embeddings and hybrid retrieval fairly?
13. How would you detect prompt injection within retrieved documents?
14. What is the difference between retrieval recall and answer groundedness?
15. How would you evaluate a new LLM provider before switching?
16. A customer wants an autonomous agent. What would you pilot first?
17. How would you version prompts, embeddings, documents and evaluation cases?
18. Why do citations not guarantee factual accuracy?

## Quotas, security, operations and FinOps
19. Multiple applications have separate non-transferable quotas. How do you allocate capacity?
20. Your LLM API returns HTTP 429 during a peak. What is the immediate action?
21. How would you forecast token costs for a pilot versus production?
22. What is the distinction between identity, roles and content authorisation?
23. A student/customer reports cross-tenant data leakage. Who does what first?
24. Which production controls would you require before exposing a portfolio RAG demo to real users?
25. How can you lower model cost without silently reducing answer quality?
26. How would you choose rate limits and fair-usage quotas for different groups?

## Outcome evidence and leadership
27. High chatbot usage correlates with better outcomes. Can you infer causation?
28. Explain random selection versus random assignment in 40 seconds.
29. What is a credible go/no-go plan for an AI pilot?
30. A sponsor wants to skip mandatory security review to hit the launch date. What do you recommend?
31. A faculty member and ITSO disagree on risk. Who decides what?
32. The team wants to add features midway through the pilot. How do you manage scope?
33. A launch is delayed due to a critical dependency. What do you personally do?
34. A team delivered a technically correct system that users reject. What did you miss?
35. How would you build accessibility into a new AI service?
36. A team asks whether to choose a local model or an external API. How do you recommend?

## Behavioural / spoken communication
37. Tell me about a project that went off course and your specific contribution.
38. Tell me about a stakeholder conflict; what did you decide?
39. You are new to the target industry. Why should the employer trust you?
40. What are your strengths and development gaps for this role?
41. Explain an architecture diagram to a non-technical panel in one minute.
42. Give a 60-second professional introduction appropriate to a real job posting.
